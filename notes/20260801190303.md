resolve_descriptor treats any `code >= KEYD_CHORD_1` (197) as a chord slot and indexes active_chords[code - 197]. The array has MAX_ACTIVE_CHORDS = 8 slots, but only three chord keycodes are reserved in keys.rs: KEYD_CHORD_1 (197), KEYD_CHORD_2 (198), KEYD_CHORD_MAX (199).

Slots 3..7 therefore alias real keycodes 200-204, which KEYCODE_TABLE defines as playcd, pausecd, scrollleft, scrollright and dashboard. With 4 or more chords held simultaneously, resolve_chord assigns chord_code = KEYD_CHORD_1 + i for i >= 3, and any genuine press of playcd/pausecd/etc. would then resolve against that active chord's descriptor instead of its own binding.

Not yet observed in practice - it needs 4+ chords active at once, which no current config reaches. Left unfixed deliberately during the Aug 2026 hot-path pass (converting active_chords from Vec to [ActiveChord; 8] preserved the behaviour exactly rather than changing it).

Fixing it means picking one of two behaviour changes: reserve five more synthetic keycodes, or cap MAX_ACTIVE_CHORDS at 3. Same family of latent aliasing bug as [[20260713215804]] - a sentinel/index space that is smaller than the code assumes.
