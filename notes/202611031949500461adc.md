The macOS and Windows Vkbd repeat threads used to thread::sleep for the delay and interval and only check the revision afterwards. A key pressed during a previous key's delay then waited a stale delay plus a full new one. They now use Condvar::wait_timeout_while on revision == rev, and send_key notifies on both arm and cancel. Separately, the daemon replaced the single global timeout_ms (decremented by elapsed time, and ticking every keyboard when it expired) with KbdRuntime.deadline per keyboard. kbd_process_events returns that keyboard's own earliest timeout, so the deadline is overwritten rather than min-merged. kbd_rt must stay index-aligned with keyboards (sync_kbd_rt in run, reset in reload).
